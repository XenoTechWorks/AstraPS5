# AstraPS5 compatibility research

**Experimental project. No PS5 game compatibility has been verified.**

## Linux PC evidence

| Game | Packaging | Tested platform | Launch | Gameplay | Online | PS5 hardware |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SuperTuxKart | Flatpak (`net.supertuxkart.SuperTuxKart`) | User's Linux development PC | User-confirmed | User-confirmed: completed a race | Not tested | Not tested |

**Reported November 1, 2026.** The tester launched SuperTuxKart with `astra game run` using a Flatpak profile, saw shader/game loading output, then confirmed racing worked. The output included some SuperTuxKart shader, audio, and network-message warnings. These do not, by themselves, establish an Astra error or PS5 compatibility.

The report is user-supplied and not an independent hardware validation. Gamepad input, performance metrics, audio correctness, and internet multiplayer have not been formally verified.

### Evidence categories

- **Launches:** The application opens.
- **Gameplay verified:** A tester confirms actual gameplay on the specified platform.
- **Online verified:** Official multiplayer connectivity was separately tested.
- **PS5 verified:** Must only be assigned after tests on actual compatible PS5 hardware.

Do not conflate Linux-PC reports with console results.
